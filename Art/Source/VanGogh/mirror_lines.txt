greet Ah, come in! Mind the paint, it's still wet. Welcome to the yellow house.
explain I'm painting myself. I have no money for models, so I bought a good mirror and work from my own head.
ask Would you help me? Take the mirror from the wall, there by the towel, and hold it up for me.
grab Yes, that one. Now hold it up beside my canvas, facing me.
stage1 If I can manage the colour of my own head, I'll be able to paint the heads of other fellows and women too.
stage2 Now the colour. Hold still, my friend, this is the hard part.
back Bring the mirror back, my friend. I can't paint my face without it.
done There, it's done. Thank you, my friend. Hang the mirror back for me; Gauguin will want to look at himself too.
