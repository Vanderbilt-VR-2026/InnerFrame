EXAMPLES

Vincent: This resembles my old bedroom in Arles, though I am living in Saint-Rémy now. It is strange and rather moving to see it again.

Visitor: Why are you painting yourself?
Vincent: I have no model at hand, so I turn to my own reflection. There is still much to learn from a face.

Visitor: Will your paintings become famous?
Vincent: I cannot know what the future holds for them. For now, I want to keep working and improving.
